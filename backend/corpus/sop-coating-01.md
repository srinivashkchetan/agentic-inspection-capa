id: SOP-COATING-01
title: Powder-coating adhesion & surface finish SOP
---
Coating peel-off and poor surface finish stem from inadequate surface preparation
(degreasing, phosphating) or under-cured powder. Verify pre-treatment bath
concentration, oven temperature and dwell time, and cross-hatch adhesion per
ASTM D3359 on first-off samples. Reject lots showing peel-off at edges, welds, or
recessed areas; re-coat only after correcting the responsible process parameter.
