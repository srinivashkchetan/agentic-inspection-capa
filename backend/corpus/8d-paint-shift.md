---
id: 8D-PAINT-SHIFT
title: Prior 8D — shift painting on molded surface
---
Root cause was fixture misalignment on the paint line causing a colour shift on
the molded surface. Corrective action: re-jig the fixtures to the datum, and add
a first-article colour check against the approved sample at the start of every
batch. Verification: no shift on 100% of first-article samples for three
consecutive batches.
