id: STD-Z1.4
title: ANSI/ASQ Z1.4 sampling & AQL acceptance
---
Acceptance is by AQL: Critical = Not Allowed, Major typically 1.5 to 2.5, Minor
4.0. A lot rejects when the defect count exceeds the accept number for the sample
size at the stated inspection level. Use the same inspection level and sampling
plan on re-inspection so acceptance decisions are comparable across lots.
