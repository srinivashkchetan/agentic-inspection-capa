id: SOP-STITCH-02
title: Stitching & thread-trim workmanship SOP
---
Broken stitches and untrimmed thread arise from incorrect stitch tension, worn
needles, or a missing end-of-line trimming step. Re-set tension to the spec for
the fabric weight, replace needles on the scheduled interval, and add a 100%
trim-and-inspect station before packing. Verify seam strength on first-article
samples and against the approved sample.
